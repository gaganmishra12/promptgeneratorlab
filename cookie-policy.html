This Cookie Policy explains how PromptGeneratorLab ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on promptgeneratorlab.com (the "Site").

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

2. Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies — required for the Site to function correctly. These can't be switched off.

Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the Site (for example, via Google Analytics), so we can improve it. These are only set if you accept them.

Advertising cookies — if we use advertising partners such as Google AdSense, they may set cookies to show and measure relevant ads. These are only set if you accept them.

3. Your choices

When you first visit the Site, you'll see a cookie notice where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can also disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not function properly if you do.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use, such as analytics or advertising providers. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
