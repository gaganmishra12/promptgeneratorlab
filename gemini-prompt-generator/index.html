Gemini prompt generator

Google — Structured prompts tuned for Gemini's multimodal and research strengths.

When to use Gemini-optimized prompts

Use Gemini-optimized prompts for multimodal tasks (text plus image or video context), research that benefits from current information, and workflows tied into the Google ecosystem.

Gemini strengths to prompt around

Multimodal understanding

Strong at reasoning across text, image, and video context in the same prompt.

Grounded research

Good integration with search grounding for tasks that need current information.

Structured extraction

Solid at structured data extraction and side-by-side comparison tasks.

Long context

Competitive long-context handling for large inputs.

Bad prompt vs. structured prompt

Bad prompt:

“Compare these two products.”

Structured:

Role: You are a shopping assistant for a budget-conscious first-time buyer.
Action: Compare Product A and Product B.
Layout/Length: Output a table with columns: Feature, Product A, Product B, Winner. Follow with a 2-sentence recommendation.

What you get back

A clean comparison table by feature, followed by a short, decisive recommendation — not a meandering paragraph comparison.
